1 Zero Account & Zero Broker Login Policy

DhanLens does not require user registration, mobile numbers, email sign-up, KYC documents (PAN/Aadhaar), bank account linking, or demat/brokerage logins. We never ask for, process, or store credentials for Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, Upstox, CDSL, NSDL, or any bank or broker.

When you tap an external shortcut button inside the app (such as viewing a stock quote on NSE India, Screener, SEBI, MFCentral, or opening your chosen broker portal), DhanLens opens the public website directly in your phone’s browser.

2 Information Stored On Your Device (Local-First)

Any personalization data you enter stays inside private application storage on your Android device — DhanLens does not upload your personal portfolio, watchlist, or tax scenarios to a developer-controlled database:

  • Watchlist, Dividend, Tax Harvest & Dip Alert Rules: Stored locally as JSON files inside private app sandbox storage.
  • Imported Portfolio Holdings: Saved strictly on your device so your portfolio health and overlap metrics load offline.
  • Optional Google Gemini API Key: If you optionally configure your own Google AI Studio API key for AI OCR parsing of screenshots/PDFs, it is stored locally using Android hardware-backed encrypted storage (expo-secure-store / Android Keystore).

3 Portfolio Import (CSV, Excel, & Optional AI OCR)

  • CSV, Excel (.xlsx / .xls) & Text Paste: Parsed 100% offline on your device. Nothing leaves your phone.
  • Optional Screenshot / PDF Statement OCR: Occurs only when you explicitly pick a specific image or PDF file and tap run with your own Gemini API key. The selected file is sent directly over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) from your device to Google’s official Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) solely to extract your holdings table. DhanLens developers operate no intermediary server and do not view, log, or retain uploaded statements.
  • Anonymous Public Market Feeds: Read-only HTTPS requests fetch public market & filing figures from public AMFI NAV feeds (api.mfapi.in), public equity chart feeds, and read-only public disclosure tables (NSE Bhavcopy EOD data, SEBI >1% shareholding & US SEC Form 13F-HR filings hosted on Supabase). No personal identifiers are attached.

5 Data Control & Data Deletion

You have full, immediate control over all data stored by DhanLens:

  • In-App One-Tap Wipe: Open Menu → Privacy Policy & Legal → Clear Local Data to permanently erase your saved watchlist, imported portfolio holdings, dividend settings, tax inputs, dip alert rules, and optional Gemini API key immediately.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling DhanLens removes all local sandbox storage files from your device.
  • Support Assistance: Because DhanLens maintains no cloud account database of personal user data, there is no remote personal profile to delete. For privacy questions or assistance, contact alokgoswami052@gmail.com (replies within 48 hours).
Age Requirement & Children’s Privacy: DhanLens provides financial calculators and capital-market analytics intended for users aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
